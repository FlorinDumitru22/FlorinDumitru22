About Our Project

🌲

Timber Frame Construction

Traditional timber frame building using locally sourced wood from sustainable Carpathian forests. A timeless construction method that's both beautiful and durable.

⚡

Off-Grid Living

Completely self-sufficient with solar panels, rainwater harvesting, and natural heating systems. Living in harmony with nature.

🤝

Community Crowdsourced

Built by and for the community. Your contribution helps create a demonstration of sustainable living and inspires others to follow.

🌍

Eco-Friendly Design

Minimal environmental impact using natural materials, passive solar design, and renewable energy. A blueprint for sustainable mountain living.
